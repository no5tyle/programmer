qStored makes apps for Scout leaders, starting with the Program Builder at program.qstored.org. This policy says what we collect when you use them, why, where it's kept, and what you can do about it. We've tried to keep it short and in plain words.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988, whether or not the Act requires a business our size to.

Using the builder without an account

You can use the Program Builder without signing in. Your program is then kept only in your own browser, on your device. It isn't sent to us.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Your email address, to sign you in (we email you a link; there's no password) and to contact you about your account.
  • Your name, if you add one. It's shown to other leaders when you share programs with them.
  • Your programs: everything you put in them, including the names and phone numbers of leaders, your group's name and logo, and any notes. Programs often hold other people's details, so please only add details the people concerned are happy for you to share.
  • Records of using the service: when your account was created and last used, and a record of each request to our servers (the time, what was asked for, your account's id, and whether it worked). These records never include what's in your programs.

Our sign-in provider, Google's Firebase Authentication, also records when you sign in and the internet address you sign in from, to protect accounts from misuse.

When paid plans start, payments will be handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details; we never see or keep them. We'll keep a record of your plan and payments.

Young people's details

The builder is for adult leaders. Please keep young people's names, contact details and other personal information out of programs: families see the program, and it's saved to your account. If young people's details are in a program, please remove them. We don't knowingly collect personal information about children.

Why we use it

  • To run the service: signing you in, saving your programs and showing them on your devices.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to send notices about your account or the service.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to find and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax records once paid plans start.

We don't sell your information, show you advertising, or use your programs for anything else. There are no analytics or tracking scripts in our apps.

Where it's kept, and who handles it for us

Your account and programs are stored by Google Cloud in Sydney, Australia, including our backups. They're encrypted while stored and while being sent. Only our own server software reads them, and only we can reach that.

To help you, and to look after the service, we can see your account's email address, name, plan and sign-in dates, and the names and dates of your programs. Our tools don't show what's in your programs. Every change we make to an account is recorded, with who made it.

We use these providers, which may handle information outside Australia:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication): your email address and sign-in records, which may be processed in the United States and other countries. Google also serves the fonts the apps use, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • GitHub hosts the app's pages, and Cloudflare (cdnjs) supplies the tools that make PDFs. Your browser contacts them when you load the app or make a PDF, as it does for any website, and they see your internet address. They don't receive your programs.
  • Stripe, once paid plans start, for payments, in the United States and other countries.
  • [Email service, once chosen: e.g. Postmark or Resend] to send emails such as invitations and receipts.

We only give your information to anyone else if the law requires it, or if you ask us to.

Your browser

The builder keeps a copy of your programs in your browser's storage, so it works quickly and without a connection, and keeps you signed in there. Signing out removes the account's programs from that browser. We don't use cookies for tracking.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and programs: until you delete them.
  • A program you delete: it goes to the bin for 30 days, so it can be brought back, and is then removed.
  • Backups: each is kept for 4 weeks, so anything deleted is gone from them within 35 days.
  • Records of requests: about 30 days.
  • Payment records, once paid plans start: as long as tax law requires (usually 5 years).

Seeing, correcting and deleting your information

  • You can change your name, and everything in your programs, yourself.
  • Download all my programs (on the Account page) gives you a copy of every program.
  • Delete my account (on the Account page) deletes your account, your programs and your sign-in, straight away.

You can also ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to correct it, by emailing [support email]. We'll reply within 30 days.

Questions and complaints

Email us at [support email]. We'll look into it and reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters to how your information is used, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
